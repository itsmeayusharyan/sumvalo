Sumvalo 0.19

- Home date strip is interactive: tap a day or step weeks with the arrows; a Today chip returns to the current date
- Net worth hide/show eye moved beside its title; the 1M/6M/1Y/All selector is one bordered pill
- Settings: Tools moved from More into Preferences
